Privacy Policy for Shield

Last updated: September 2026

Kharitonenkov Maksim ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Shield mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

Shield is designed to prioritize user privacy. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect your personal name, physical address, or phone number. Any scenario inputs entered by users are processed in transient memory solely to produce text responses.

2. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party service providers that may collect information used to identify your device or process transactions:

  • Google Play Services: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements to non-paying users. AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (such as GAID) in accordance with Google policies. AdMob Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Used for processing in-app purchases and managing subscriptions. Collects anonymous app user IDs and purchase receipt tokens. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • AI Processing (OpenAI API): User prompt scenarios are securely transmitted to backend AI infrastructure solely to generate responses. Prompts are not used to train global AI models. OpenAI Privacy Policy

3. Log Data and Analytics

In case of an error or crash in the app, we may collect technical diagnostics (Log Data). This may include your device model, operating system version, app settings, and timestamp of the incident. This data is used exclusively to troubleshoot stability issues.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Shield does not require user accounts, we do not store personal account profiles on permanent servers. Transient prompt texts are discarded after generation. If you wish to delete any cached local application data, you can do so at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application. You may also contact us to request data deletion.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We advise you to review this page occasionally for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon posting to this URL.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy or data handling practices, contact us at: maxkharito@gmail.com.
